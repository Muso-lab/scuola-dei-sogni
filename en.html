Pedagogical Approach

Our Method: Seamless 0–6 Integrated Educational Continuity

Scuola dei Sogni is an early childhood educational institution authorized and accredited by Rome Municipio III, offering seamless 0–6 educational continuity between nursery and kindergarten.

Our methodology follows an integrated 0–6 system designed to give every child equal opportunities to cultivate their highest potential in social relations, independence, creativity, and natural learning.

Autonomy & Confidence

We nurture active initiative: “I can do it myself”.

Natural Early Bilingualism

Early English immersion through songs, storytelling, and playful daily interaction with native educators.

Empathetic Connection

Warm empathetic listening among peers and specialized teachers.

Active Hands-On Learning

Hands-on labs and direct tactile discovery with nature and materials.

🌟 Does early bilingualism confuse young children?

Not at all. During early childhood, the human brain possesses extraordinary phonetic plasticity. Through natural daily immersion guided by native English educators, children absorb rhythms, pronunciation, and vocabulary with the same effortless joy as their mother tongue, fostering cognitive flexibility and confident open-mindedness.

Enrichment Workshops

Specialist Workshops & Programs at Scuola dei Sogni

Inspiring activities that nurture personal growth, creativity, and joyful learning in every child.

🇬🇧

English with Native Educator

Immersive daily activities led by native English educators: effortless, natural acquisition of phonetic rhythms, vocabulary, and grammar through interactive songs, games, and storytelling.

🌱

Educational Botanical Garden

Hands-on contact with the earth: children observe the cycle of seasons and plant life, learning shared care, patience, and eco-friendly reverence for nature.

🩰

Dance & Movement Workshop

Movement education and physical expression enabling children to express emotions, imagination, musical rhythm, and graceful body coordination freely.

Psychomotricity & Motor Skills

A safe, encouraging space designed to cultivate gross motor skills and spatial awareness, reinforcing personal autonomy, peer connection, and physical confidence.

An inspiring dramatic adventure conducted entirely in English: role-playing, character dress-up, and expressive storytelling to foster empathy, public speaking poise, and verbal fluency.

Music & Rhythm Workshop

Themed Pajama Parties

Our Guiding Values

Our Educational Philosophy

Scuola dei Sogni pursues educational values that continuously inspire children toward active discovery through intentional, creative experiences.

01

Meaningful Personal Relationships

Among peers and adults across daily life experiences, rooted in mutual respect, empathy, and heartfelt trust.

02

The Power and Value of Play

In all its expressions: an essential foundation of joyful discovery, emotional growth, and peer socialization.

03

Observing Nature and the World

Guiding a child’s natural curiosity through structured, wonder-filled journeys of sensory exploration and active discovery.

04

Developing Unique Identity

Supporting each child in serenely building a positive self-identity: “Who am I? What are my natural talents?”

05

Self-Confidence & Security

Empowering children with self-assurance and joy in their own abilities, making them proud of their personal growth.

06

Attaining Independence

“I can do it myself!”: teachers act as attentive guides encouraging initiative and practical competence through hands-on exploration.
